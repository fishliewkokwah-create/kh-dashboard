True alkalinity doesn't change with temperature, but the underlying pH/CO₂ chemistry does -- so readings can drift slightly if test temperature differs from your calibration temperature. This corrects for that empirically, using your own data rather than a generic textbook constant.

How to derive your coefficient: after calibrating above, wait for the tank to naturally reach a different temperature (e.g. day vs night, or heater cycling), then cross-check with your manual kit again at that new temperature. If the manual kit still shows the same true KH but this unit now reads differently, compute:
coeff = (unit_KH_error / unit_KH_at_calibration) / (new_temp − cal_temp)
